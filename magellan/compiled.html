Drag to pan, scroll/pinch to zoom. Two characters: WASD or the left joystick walks the blue one, arrow keys or the right joystick walks the red one. Press T then click the map to teleport whichever is nearer there. Press F to toggle fog of war, X to forget explored ground - each character explores its own ground, and the map shows whatever either one has seen. Every tile and cliff face is looked up from a precompiled bitmap and blitted as-is.
